# 02 — Branching

## Overview
Branches let you experiment without disturbing the main line of work. This exercise covers creating, switching between, and comparing branches.

## Your Task
1. Create a new branch called `feature-from-class`.
2. Switch to it and add a small note file.
3. Use `git branch` and `git log` to compare branches and commits.

## Tip
Branches are just pointers to commits — updating your local branch is cheap and fast.
